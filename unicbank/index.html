UNIC Bank

Reduced time-to-value in mobile banking — a UX/UI redesign of the registration, account-opening and transfer flows for a B2C fintech concept.

Role
UX/UI Designer
Type
B2C fintech concept
Platform
iOS mobile app
Duration
4 weeks

Problem

UNIC originally started as a website for international money transfers. As the company expanded into mobile banking, the new app needed to support registration and account opening, UNIC and third-party card management, and domestic and international transfers.

The challenge was to introduce these new services and grow the customer base without disrupting the experience of existing users who primarily came to send money.

Competitor analysis

I analyzed three popular fintech apps — Wise, Revolut, and N26 — to see how they approached the key flows UNIC needed, identify their strengths and weaknesses, and explore opportunities for improvement.

Interviews

Based on the competitor analysis, I developed four hypotheses and tested them through user interviews to understand which opportunities addressed real user needs. Three hypotheses were confirmed, while one was not.

HypothesisResultNote
Split registration and bank account opening Confirmed Users wanted to explore before giving full KYC data.
Home screen as progressive onboarding Confirmed Users needed clearer guidance after registration.
Scheduled transfers with confirmation control Confirmed · Insight Users wanted automation without silent money movement.
Quick transfers to recent recipients Not confirmed Recent transaction history mattered more than quick access to recipients alone.

Decision 1

Split registration from bank account opening. The user can sign up, browse and use lightweight features without committing to a full KYC flow up front. Account opening becomes contextual.

Before~10 min to value

  1. Sign up
  2. ID verification
  3. Address proof
  4. Account opening
  5. First useful action

After~3 min to value

  1. Short registration
  2. Guided home screen
  3. First useful action
  4. Account opening later

Decision 2

Home screen as progressive onboarding. The home screen is a dashboard and a discovery layer at the same time. It surfaces the next meaningful action based on what the user has already done.

Decision 3

Scheduled transfers with confirmation. Users can choose to receive a confirmation notification before each scheduled transfer is executed. Automation stays convenient, while users remain in control.

Final concept

The final concept combined UNIC’s existing money transfer services with broader mobile banking functionality.

  1. Lightweight registration, with full KYC required only when opening a bank account
  2. A progressive home screen that adapts as users unlock banking features
  3. Domestic and international transfers, including scheduled payments with optional confirmation
  4. UNIC and third-party card management
  5. Recent transaction history for easier access to previous payments

Results

The final prototype was tested with users to validate the redesigned flows. The results showed an estimated reduction in time to first useful interaction from ~10 to ~3 minutes.

Testing also showed that users understood what to do next from the home screen, recognized account opening as a separate step rather than an upfront requirement, and understood that scheduled transfers required confirmation before execution.
